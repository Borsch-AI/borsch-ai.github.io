Terms of Service

Last updated: 19 November 2026

Borsch AI is a channel of short animated videos about artificial intelligence. The "Borsch AI publisher" is a private tool used only by the channel's owner to upload the channel's own videos to the owner's own accounts on YouTube, Instagram and TikTok. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

Use of the tool

Only the owner signs in to the tool, with their own accounts, and only to publish videos made for the Borsch AI channel. Each video is reviewed and approved by the owner before it is published.

Content

The videos are original animations made by the owner with their own characters. They are labelled as AI-generated where a platform asks for it. Facts in the videos come from public sources named in each post.

Platforms

Publishing follows the terms and community guidelines of YouTube, Instagram and TikTok. The tool only uses the official APIs of these platforms and only the permissions needed to upload videos and read the channel's own comments.

No warranty

The videos are for information and entertainment. They are not professional advice.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

What the tool stores

Only the access tokens of the owner's own accounts, kept on the owner's own computer so the tool can upload the owner's videos. Nothing is sold, shared or sent to anyone else.

Viewers and commenters

The tool does not collect personal data about viewers. To moderate the channel, it can read public comments on the channel's own videos and hide spam or abuse; commenters' names are not stored, and comment text is kept only as anonymous topic ideas.

Third parties

Videos and captions are sent only to the platforms the owner publishes to (YouTube, Instagram, TikTok), under their own privacy policies.

Deletion

The owner can revoke the tool's access at any time in each platform's settings; the stored tokens are then deleted from the owner's computer.
